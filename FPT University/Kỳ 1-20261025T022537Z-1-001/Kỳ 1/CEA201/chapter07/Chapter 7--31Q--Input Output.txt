b. Laser
c. Dot matrix

Answer: d

31. Which of the following is not a characteristic of inkjet printers?
a. Produce high-quality color output
b. Use liquid ink cartridges
c. Produce fast printing speeds
d. Can print on a variety of media types
Answer: c

32. Which of the following is not a characteristic of laser printers?
a. Produce high-quality black and white output
b. Use toner cartridges
c. Produce fast printing speeds
d. Can print on a variety of media types
Answer: d

33. Which of the following is not a characteristic of dot matrix printers?
a. Produce high-quality color output
b. Use ribbons to transfer ink onto paper
c. Produce slow printing speeds
d. Can print on continuous paper
Answer: a

d. OLED

Answer: a
